# Content System

## Content Position

Create content around one enemy: overheated sleep.

Do not lead with "sleep optimization." Lead with immediate bedroom discomfort.

## Hook Bank

1. If you flip your pillow all night, this is for you.
2. Your room is not the only reason you wake up hot.
3. Stop building a winter bed for a summer body.
4. Hot sleepers need a different bedding setup.
5. The cool side of the pillow should last longer than five seconds.
6. If your blanket makes you sweat, the problem is the setup.
7. Three upgrades for people who sleep hot.
8. I rebuilt my bed for summer sleep.
9. This is what I would buy before replacing my mattress.
10. The cheapest way to make your bed feel cooler.

## Short Video Scripts

### Script 1: Pillow Flip Problem

Shot 1: person flips pillow repeatedly.

Voiceover: If you flip your pillow all night looking for the cool side, your sleep setup is fighting you.

Shot 2: show cooling pillow cover or pillow.

Voiceover: Start with the surface touching your face. Cool-touch fabric and a washable cover make the bed feel fresher without replacing the mattress.

Shot 3: product close-up.

Text: Built for hot sleepers.

CTA: Build your Hot Sleeper Reset Kit.

### Script 2: Too Hot Under Blankets

Shot 1: person kicks blanket off.

Voiceover: Some people want blanket comfort without waking up sweaty.

Shot 2: show lightweight cooling blanket.

Voiceover: A lighter, breathable layer is the first swap I would make.

Shot 3: show full bed setup.

CTA: Start with the cooling layer.

### Script 3: Apartment Sleep Kit

Shot 1: street noise or thin-wall apartment.

Voiceover: If your bedroom is bright, noisy, and warm, do not start with supplements.

Shot 2: show mask, white-noise device, cooling pillow cover.

Voiceover: Fix the room first: cooler surface, less light, less noise.

CTA: Build the kit.

### Script 4: Bundle Demo

Shot 1: show all products laid out.

Voiceover: This is the hot-sleeper reset: cooler pillow surface, lighter blanket, blackout mask, optional white noise.

Shot 2: make the bed.

Voiceover: One setup, not random sleep gadgets.

CTA: Shop the reset kit.

## 14-Day Posting Plan

Post 2 videos per day:

- Morning: problem/solution demo
- Evening: bedroom reset or product close-up

Repeat winners with new hooks. Do not invent new angles until one gets signal.

Signals to track:

- 3-second hold
- Comments from hot sleepers
- Saves
- Profile visits
- Link clicks
- Email signups

## UGC Creator Brief

Product: Hot Sleeper Reset Kit

Target viewer: people who wake up warm, flip pillows, and dislike heavy bedding.

Required shots:

- Opening problem scene
- Product close-up
- Bed setup
- Face/hand fabric reaction
- Final clean bedroom shot

Do not say:

- cured my insomnia
- fixed my anxiety
- medical night sweats
- guaranteed better sleep

Say:

- feels cooler
- lighter bedding setup
- more comfortable warm nights
- better bedroom setup for hot sleepers
