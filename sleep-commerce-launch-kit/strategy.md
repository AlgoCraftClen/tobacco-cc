# CoolRest Supply Strategy

## Executive Decision

Build a narrow store for hot sleepers, not a broad sleep store.

The first version sells a cooling sleep setup: pillow, blanket or comforter, eye mask, and white-noise accessory. The store should feel like a solution for one bedroom problem, not a catalog.

## Why This Wedge

Current sleep-product coverage shows ongoing buyer interest in cooling pillows, cooling comforters, cooling weighted blankets, white-noise machines, blackout masks, and breathable bedding. These products are easier to demonstrate visually than supplements, less regulated than medical sleep aids, and more bundle-friendly than a single gadget.

## Customer

Primary buyer: adults who sleep hot and wake up uncomfortable.

Likely segments:

- Hot sleepers in warm climates
- Apartment renters without strong AC
- Couples where one partner sleeps warmer
- People who like blanket weight but overheat
- Travel sleepers dealing with noisy or bright rooms

The first customer avatar:

Name: Jordan

Situation: runs warm at night, flips the pillow, kicks off the blanket, wakes up uncomfortable, and wants a fix without replacing the mattress.

Buying trigger: summer heat, poor AC, moving into a warmer bedroom, partner complaints, travel, or seeing a creator demonstrate the "cool side of the pillow" problem.

## Offer Architecture

Lead product:

**Hot Sleeper Reset Kit**

Included:

- Cooling pillow cover or pillow
- Lightweight cooling blanket, throw, or comforter
- Contoured blackout sleep mask

Upsells:

- White-noise machine
- Travel cooling kit
- Extra pillow cover pair

Future bundle:

**Apartment Sleep Kit**

Included:

- White-noise machine
- Door draft/noise strip
- Blackout mask
- Cooling pillow cover

## Product Requirements

Reject products that fail these filters:

- Supplier rating below 4.7 or weak review volume
- No clear product photos or video
- Long shipping estimates without tracking
- Medical claims in supplier listing
- Complex sizing or high return risk
- Fragile electronics without clear warranty
- Heavy blankets with poor shipping economics

Prefer products with:

- Demonstrable visual hook
- Repeatable content angle
- Low defect risk
- Lightweight shipping
- Bundling logic
- Washable cover or easy-care story
- Neutral colors
- Price room for 2.5x to 4x markup

## Initial Product Shortlist

Priority 1: cooling pillow cover or cool-touch pillow

Reason: strong pain point, simple demo, low shipping risk, easy before/after creative.

Target landed cost: $5 to $16

Target price: $24 to $49

Priority 2: lightweight cooling blanket or cooling throw

Reason: strong bundle anchor, visual fabric demo, summer relevance.

Target landed cost: $12 to $35

Target price: $49 to $89

Priority 3: contoured blackout mask

Reason: cheap AOV booster, travel angle, low defect risk.

Target landed cost: $2 to $8

Target price: $14 to $29

Priority 4: white-noise machine

Reason: clear adjacent sleep problem, but test carefully because electronics create defect and return risk.

Target landed cost: $8 to $20

Target price: $29 to $59

## Pricing Model

Minimum target:

- Gross margin before ads: 55% or better
- Average order value: $59 or higher
- Contribution margin after payment fees and shipping: 35% or better before ads

Launch prices:

- Cooling Pillow Cover Pair: $29
- Cooling Sleep Mask: $17
- Cooling Blanket: $69
- Hot Sleeper Reset Kit: $89
- White Noise Mini: $39

Bundle pricing should make the kit the obvious choice.

## Compliance Position

Do not claim to cure insomnia, anxiety, medical night sweats, sleep apnea, or health conditions.

Use product-experience copy:

- "cool-touch feel"
- "breathable comfort"
- "designed for warm sleepers"
- "helps your bed feel lighter and cooler"
- "blocks light"
- "masks background noise"

Do not use before/after medical claims, exaggerated sleep guarantees, or invented testimonials.

## Channel Strategy

Phase 1: organic proof and store readiness

- TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts
- 2 short videos per day for 14 days
- Product demos, problem/solution videos, bedroom setup videos
- No paid ads until samples are received

Phase 2: micro-budget paid testing

- Only after samples and working checkout
- Start with $10 to $20 per day
- Test 3 creatives, not 10 products
- Kill ads that cannot get cheap add-to-cart or checkout interest

Phase 3: conversion improvement

- Add reviews only from real buyers
- Add FAQ based on real objections
- Add email capture
- Improve bundle economics

## 30-Day Execution Sequence

Day 1:

- Approve brand name
- Create business email
- Choose payment processor
- Research 20 products using the scorecard

Days 2-3:

- Shortlist 5 suppliers
- Message suppliers
- Build product page around placeholder products
- Create first 20 videos scripts

Days 4-7:

- Order samples
- Publish static site with payment disabled or waitlist mode
- Post organic content daily
- Build email capture

Days 8-14:

- Receive supplier responses
- Choose first products
- Create product photos/videos from samples if available
- Finalize payment links

Days 15-21:

- Soft launch to organic traffic
- Manually fulfill first orders
- Track objections and conversion issues

Days 22-30:

- Test small paid budget if product quality is confirmed
- Improve product page
- Add second bundle or upsell only if first offer converts

## Kill Criteria

Stop or pivot if:

- No supplier can provide tracked shipping under 12 business days
- Product quality is not sample-worthy
- Content gets no saves, comments, profile visits, or clicks after 30 posts
- Paid test cannot generate add-to-cart interest at a sustainable cost
- Refund/defect risk is high

## Strong Adjacent Opportunity

If cooling sleep products look commoditized after supplier research, pivot to **travel sleep kits for hot/light sleepers**.

Reason: travel has stronger urgency, smaller products, easier bundles, and lower shipping complexity.

Travel kit:

- Cooling eye mask
- Earplugs or white-noise mini
- Compact cooling pillow cover
- Travel pouch
