## Compliance Constraints

FTC influencer disclosure guidance:

- Disclose paid, gifted, discounted, employment, family, or other material connections.
- Put disclosures where people will see them.
- Do not make claims about product experiences you have not had.
- Do not make health-treatment claims without proof.
- Source: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers

Google Merchant Center misrepresentation guidance:

- Be upfront and honest.
- Do not present false identity, contact information, affiliation, or delivery capability.
- Do not offer products you cannot deliver.
- Source: https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/6150127?hl=en
