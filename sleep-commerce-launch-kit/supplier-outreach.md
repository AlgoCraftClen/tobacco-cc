# Supplier Outreach

## Supplier Qualification Questions

Send this before choosing a supplier:

Hello, I am preparing to launch a small sleep-comfort brand in the United States. I am interested in your product and want to confirm the details before ordering samples.

1. Can you provide tracked shipping to the United States?
2. What is the typical delivery time after order processing?
3. Do you support dropshipping with no invoice or marketplace branding in the package?
4. Can you provide product photos or videos for marketing use?
5. What is your defect or replacement policy?
6. Can you handle 10, 50, and 100 orders per week if the product performs well?
7. Are the materials certified or tested, such as OEKO-TEX, if applicable?
8. Can you provide the exact package size and weight?
9. Is there a minimum order quantity for custom branding later?
10. Can I order one sample today?

Thank you.

## Supplier Scoring

Score each supplier from 1 to 5:

- Shipping speed
- Tracking reliability
- Product quality evidence
- Response quality
- Branding flexibility
- Replacement policy
- Margin room
- Content asset quality

Reject any supplier that dodges shipping, tracking, defects, or packaging questions.

## Sample Order Checklist

Before paid ads:

- Order one sample of every lead product
- Record unboxing
- Test fabric feel and quality
- Check stitching, smell, packaging, and defects
- Wash if washable
- Photograph under clean daylight
- Confirm shipping time matches promise
- Confirm no supplier invoice or marketplace branding appears in package

## Product Page Data Needed

For each approved product:

- Product title
- 3 core benefits
- Material details
- Size/dimensions
- Care instructions
- Shipping time
- Return policy
- Product photos
- Product demo video
- Supplier backup option
