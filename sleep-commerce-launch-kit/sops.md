# Operating SOPs

## Daily Operator Checklist

1. Check new orders.
2. Place supplier orders manually if checkout is live.
3. Update order tracker with supplier tracking.
4. Reply to customer emails.
5. Post two short videos.
6. Log content performance.
7. Research or validate one new supplier/product.

## Product Research SOP

1. Add candidate product to `product-research-scorecard.csv`.
2. Fill in supplier, cost, shipping, rating, reviews, and risk fields.
3. Score product from 1 to 5 for demo potential, margin, return risk, compliance risk, and bundle fit.
4. Reject products with unclear shipping or weak proof.
5. Shortlist no more than five products.
6. Message suppliers.
7. Order samples before paid traffic.

## Manual Fulfillment SOP

1. Customer places order through Stripe or PayPal.
2. Copy customer shipping details to fulfillment tracker.
3. Place supplier order using customer shipping address.
4. Use no-invoice/no-branding note when available.
5. Record supplier order ID.
6. Add tracking when available.
7. Send customer shipping update.
8. Monitor delivery.

## Customer Support Templates

### Order Confirmation

Subject: Your CoolRest Supply order is confirmed

Thanks for your order. We are preparing your cooling sleep setup now. You will receive tracking as soon as it is available.

### Shipping Update

Subject: Your CoolRest Supply order has shipped

Your order is on the way. Tracking: [TRACKING LINK]

Delivery estimates can vary slightly by carrier, but we will monitor the order until it arrives.

### Delay Response

Subject: Update on your CoolRest Supply order

Your order is still moving through the shipping process, but it is taking longer than expected. I am checking the tracking and will keep you updated until it arrives.

### Refund/Replacement

Subject: We will make this right

I am sorry the order arrived with an issue. Please send one clear photo of the item and packaging so I can confirm the problem and arrange the right next step.

## Weekly Review

Every 7 days:

- Review top 10 videos by views and saves
- Review clicks and email signups
- Review abandoned checkout or payment-link data
- Review supplier response quality
- Kill weak products
- Improve the product page FAQ
- Choose next week's content hooks
