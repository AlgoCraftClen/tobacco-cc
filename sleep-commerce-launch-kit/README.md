# Sleep Commerce Launch Kit

Mission: build a lean, profit-seeking e-commerce business in the sleep niche, starting with hot-sleeper comfort.

## Strategic Decision

Start with a single wedge: **cooler sleep for hot sleepers**.

Do not launch as a generic sleep store. Generic sleep is too broad, too competitive, and too easy to make weak health claims. Hot-sleeper comfort is better because the problem is concrete: waking up sweaty, flipping pillows, tossing off blankets, sleeping badly in warm rooms, and needing a cooler setup without buying a new mattress.

## Brand

Working brand: **CoolRest Supply**

Positioning: practical cooling sleep upgrades for people who run hot at night.

Tone: calm, clean, direct, not clinical.

Core promise: build a cooler sleep setup without replacing your mattress.

Avoid claims like: cures insomnia, treats night sweats, reduces anxiety, improves medical sleep disorders, guaranteed better sleep.

Use claims like: cooler feel, breathable materials, lightweight comfort, quieter room, less tossing from heat, easier bedtime setup.

## Initial Offer

Lead offer: **The Hot Sleeper Reset Kit**

Bundle structure:

1. Cooling pillow cover or cooling pillow
2. Lightweight cooling blanket or comforter
3. Contoured sleep mask
4. Optional white-noise device as an upsell

Why bundle: dropshipping single-item margins are fragile. A kit raises average order value, gives content a clear story, and makes the store feel intentional.

## Minimum Viable Store

Use a static site first:

- GitHub Pages for hosting
- Stripe Payment Links or PayPal buttons for checkout
- Manual order routing to suppliers at first
- Google Sheets or the included CSV for product research
- Manual customer support email

No Shopify subscription until product-market signal exists.
