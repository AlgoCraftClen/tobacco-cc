Business Rules
First shipment excluded
The proof-of-concept loss is not carried forward.
Starting clean with SHP-002.
Clenny product investment
$501.00 starts as Clenny's investment for the Copenhagen shipment.
Editable if cash contribution changes.
Clanny product investment
The remainder of the product cost belongs to Clanny.
Formula: total product cost minus Clenny investment.
Own operations entered separately
Clanny enters Clanny costs; Clenny enters Clenny costs.
Gas, rental, freight, pickup, loading, etc.
No even split on operations
Operational costs are not automatically split 50/50.
Each person's approved ops increase their contribution basis.
Approved business ops only
Personal expenses should not be included.
Do not let random lunch receipts become corporate finance.
Product taken personally
Enter as sale/withdrawal, not as operations.
This prevents product taken from hiding inside operations.
Final payout method
Payout = contribution reimbursement + profit share.
Profit share is based on total approved contribution basis.
Cash collection matters
Whoever collects customer money is tracked separately.
Settlement balances compare payout due versus cash held.
Inventory math
2 boxes = 12 cases = 216 rolls = 1,080 cans.
Based on 6 cases/box, 18 rolls/case, 5 cans/roll.
Target sale price
Workbook uses $12/can as projected sales price.
Change this if actual selling price changes.
